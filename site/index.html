Install in 2 minutes

The beta isn't signed by Apple yet, so macOS asks you to confirm once.

  1. Download PromptFlow.dmg with the button above and open it.
  2. Drag PromptFlow into Applications, then open it from Applications.
  3. macOS says it can't verify the app. Click Done, then go to System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway. Confirm with your password.
  4. Sign in with your email. Enter the 6-digit code from the email, or click its link.
  5. Allow Microphone, Accessibility and Input Monitoring when asked. They let PromptFlow hear you, paste into other apps and notice the Fn key.
  6. Open any app, hold Fn, talk, and let go. Watch the island at the top of the screen.

Questions

Is it free?

Yes, during the beta, with a fair-use limit per person per day.

Where does my voice go?

Audio goes to our server to be transcribed and rewritten, then is discarded. Your history and memory are tied to your account and you can delete them, or your whole account, from Settings. Private mode keeps everything on your Mac.

Why does macOS warn me when opening it?

Apps from outside the App Store need a paid Apple signature to skip that step. We'll add it soon; until then, "Open Anyway" is the safe one-time way through.

An update asked for permissions again

Beta builds have a new signature each time, so macOS can forget Microphone and Accessibility. Re-allow them in System Settings → Privacy & Security; PromptFlow shows you where.

iPhone and Android?

Both are built and coming next. Mac comes first.
